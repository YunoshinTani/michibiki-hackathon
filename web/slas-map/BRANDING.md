# みちびき測位モニターのロゴ

tanishi.tech の配色と形を参考に、測位衛星をモチーフとする透過ロゴを組み込みの画像生成ツールで作成しました。`brand-icon.png` は見出し用の512px PNG、`favicon-32x32.png` はブラウザーのタブ用です。どちらも同じ生成画像を縮小したものです。

フッターの `brand-light.png` と `brand-dark.png` は `V:\5_server\tani-server1-dev\web\sites\home\public` から無加工でコピーしています。

## 生成プロンプト

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create a new single transparent icon for the Michibiki positioning monitor, in the existing tanishi.tech raster brand family. Input images are style references only: 1 is the home identity, 2 is its microcontroller sibling. Subject: a small friendly navigation satellite tilted diagonally, built from a chunky rounded blue central body, two clearly recognizable rounded solar wings (one blue and one green), a compact yellow curved accent and a small red signal accent. One white inset on the central body carries just two small dark gray dots, echoing the family mascot. Smooth flat flowing shapes, strong simple silhouette with very few details and exactly the family colors blue #4285F4, green #34A853, yellow #FBBC05, red #EA4335, white and dark gray. One centered compact square icon, fills around 83% of the canvas, recognizable at 24px. Genuine alpha transparency outside the logo; no text, numbers, background, orbit trails, scenery, globe, gradients, shadows, texture, outlines around the entire shape, watermark or mockup.
```
